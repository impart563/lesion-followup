Review boundary. This is a software review aid for manually supplied measurements, not an overall oncology response assessment or a treatment recommendation. The caller confirms target selection, measurement axes, organ grouping and comparable acquisition.

Missing observations stay visible. Explicit too-small observations use a documented 5.0 mm default. NE visits do not update the nadir. Percentages are truncated display values; exact integer comparisons drive categories.
